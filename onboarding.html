Welcome to Talon

Talon takes over a stuck support conversation, figures out when it's talking to a bot instead of a person, and keeps pushing until a real human responds.

How it reaches people

Talon emails the company directly on your behalf and keeps the entire back-and-forth in one thread. You never have to relay messages yourself.

How it decides

Every reply gets read and judged as bot or human, using Caspian's multi-channel agent framework as the backbone that connects Talon to email, Telegram, and everywhere else it needs to reach.

How you stay in the loop

The moment a real person responds, or the issue is actually resolved, Talon pings you. Not before.
